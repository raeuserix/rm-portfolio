BOOTCAMP PROJECTS

While completing my George Washington Univeristy UI/UX bootcamp certification (February 2024 - August 2024), I had the opportunity to work with like-minded peers to complete projects ranging from responsive web design, to mobile application workflow design, to front-end development work.

My goal while taking this course was to navigate through the full UI/UX design workflow successfully, implement solutions that could improve existing products and/or help our users complete desired tasks, and overall, to learn.


MR. J'S BAGELS AND DELI:

Desktop Website Redesign

GW UX/UI Bootcamp Case Study

When visiting the Shenandoah area, I definitely recommend checking out Mr. J's Bagels and Deli if you're in the mood for food. Our concept for a website redesign targets an easier way to scan the website's menu, new branding, and a straightforward in-website ordering process.


LITTLE WANDERERS NYC:

Responsive Website Redesign

GW UX/UI Bootcamp Case Study

Calling all cat lovers! Need an easier way to find all the necessary information you are looking for in the Little Wanderers NYC website? Check out our new concept for the non-profit's redesigned website, which includes a more direct donation process, detailed adoptable cat information, and all of your cat support needs!
